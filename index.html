How to Play Beatdle
  • Guess the mystery song in 6 tries. You always start with its exact BPM.
  • Every wrong guess unlocks a new clue: first letter after Guess 1, artist after Guess 2, and word/letter count after Guess 3.
  • After each guess, check the feedback pills: Artist (exact match), Title (exact match or shares words), and BPM (exact, within ±5 BPM, or higher/lower ↑ / ↓).
  • A new Daily puzzle unlocks every day, or switch to Practice anytime for unlimited random tracks.
